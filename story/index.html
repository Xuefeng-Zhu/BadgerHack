About This Data

Transparency matters. Here's what you should know about this dataset:

  • Source: All data comes from EFF's Badger Sett project — automated scans of top websites using Privacy Badger.
  • Detection method: Privacy Badger uses heuristic-based detection. It identifies tracking by observing third-party domains that appear across multiple first-party sites. This means some trackers may be missed, and some non-tracking third parties may be flagged.
  • Scan scope: Scans cover the top ~5,000–8,000 websites from the Tranco list. This represents popular sites but not the entire web.
  • Browser variation: Results vary by browser (Firefox, Chrome, Edge) due to different built-in privacy protections.
  • Point-in-time snapshots: Each scan captures a moment. Websites change their tracker configurations frequently.
  • Not all tracking is equal: Some third-party requests are functional (fonts, CDNs). Privacy Badger's heuristics aim to distinguish tracking from non-tracking, but the line isn't always clear.
